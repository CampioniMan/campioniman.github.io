This was the first project I created from scratch that was actually implemented in real life and worked wonderfully on the first attempt! Lucky indeed. Let's start by adding the necessary context of the whole problem the project solves, story time!

## The Problem

I have a cousing who owns a YouTube channel since 2020 containing a strong element of role play with lots of characters with different personalities, but he is only one person and can only do so much before burning out from writing scripts, checking continuity, recording, editting, dubbing, double-checking everything, etc. The pain he felt the most was at the very start of the chain, writing and checking continuity, because he has an on-going series with 4 seasons fully produced and published and a 5th haulted by stress, which added a lot of complexity to creating and adding new stories, since they need to make sense to the audience who knows fine details from each of the characters.

## The Solution

I helped him with a simple AI solution that enables him to focus on what's more important at the very beginning: writing and continuity. So a repository was created with a very specific structure that lists facts, relationships, environments, universe rules, in a intuitive way for humans and AI to read/write: Markdown. I said humans, but I meant programmers.
